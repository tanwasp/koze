      Share clips to help build Kreol speech recognition

      Very little Kreol Morisien speech has been written down, which is why speech models struggle with it. Each clip you share, with your corrected transcript, helps fix that.

      What's sent
      Only the clips you choose to share: the audio, the model's transcript, your correction, your feedback, and any details below. Not your name, email or location.
      How it's used
      To train and test the Koze speech models.
      Where it's kept
      In a private Supabase database that only the Koze maintainer can read.
      Changing your mind
      Withdraw any clip from "Shared from this device" below the transcript, in this browser. From another device, open an issue. A clip can't be recalled from public datasets that were already downloaded.
